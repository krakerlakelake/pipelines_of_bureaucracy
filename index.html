Layers

Datasets on the map. The view buttons above the map switch these for you; tick more than one to see them together.

Court cases

Show court cases as dots at the court house, or shade each court's area blue, darker the more cases it has in the time window. Courts that share an area are added together.

Filters

Numbers count events in the current time window. An event stays visible while at least one of its categories is ticked.

How to use

  • Time window: drag the bright box in the timeline to move it, drag its edges to resize it, or drag on empty space to draw a new one. ← → step it forward and back.
  • Highlight a period: click a bar. Events from that period are lit up on the map and the rest are dimmed. Click it again or press Esc to clear.
  • Event details: click a dot. Its events are listed on the right and marked in white on the timeline.
  • Zoom the timeline: mouse wheel. Scroll sideways or drag the date axis to pan, and double-click to reset.
  • Dots grow with the number of events at the same location: a dot's area is proportional to its events, on the same scale for every category.
  • Court areas: in the Layers panel, switch court cases from points to areas. Each court's area is shaded in proportion to its cases in the time window (the legend at the top left gives the scale); click an area to list its cases.
